## Abstract 

Deployed language model safeguards (safety fine-tuning, filtering, unlearning) vary by principal only outside the model weights: filters are reconfigured, tiers are mul-tiplied, and artefacts are reissued; inside one set of weights every request meets the same model configuration. This motivates us to define capability-gated deployment :per-principal access control inside one set of weights, whose configurations form a lattice — meets accumulate a principal’s restrictions and joins pool a coalition’s reach. We instantiate it by sparse rank gating over an existing nested-factorisation mechanism, guide profile search with one-pass attribution, and read every result once from a pre-registered held-out split. Security composes: provably at meets under a monotone-elicitation assumption we falsify pointwise. In two lineages the median held-out meet deepens suppression; the one effect surviving correction strengthens it. Utility does not: individually harmless profiles can compose to retention and fluency damage, and no compositional bound exists. 

## 1 Introduction 

A deployed language model is one endpoint and one configuration: a capability withheld from an anonymous user is withheld from a vetted defender in the same deployment. Protection theory has held since the 1970s that authority should instead be indexed to a principal, so that a right can be granted, narrowed, or revoked for one subject without altering it for every other [Lampson, 1974, Graham and Denning, 1972]. The mismatch is newly consequential for language models: frontier capability is rising on a steep, well-characterised trend [Kwa et al., 2025], dangerous-capability evaluation has become a precondition of release [Shevlane et al., 2023, Phuong et al., 2024], and the domains at issue – biology, chemistry, offensive cybersecurity [Li et al., 2024] – are exactly those in which different principals warrant different capability. The safeguards that ship are global by construction: post-training alignment shapes one output distribution for every request thereafter [Ouyang et al., 2022, Bai et al., 2022, Rafailov et al., 2023]; unlearning and concept erasure edit the weights, but the edited weights are the single artefact every user receives [Li et al., 2024, Belrose et al., 2023, Meng et al., 2022]; and classifiers mediate every request against a policy beside the weights – reconfigurable per tenant, but only over what the weights still emit. Per-principal variation is thus bought outside any one set of weights – recon-figured filters, tiered and identity-verified access, restricted releases, managed-access frameworks [Carter and Butchello, 2026, Bloomfield et al., 2026] – at the granularity of whole accounts and whole models: each distinct authority is a distinct filter policy over intact capability, or a distinct artefact to train, evaluate, and maintain [Roland et al., 2026]. The caveat is known – filtering leaves the underlying computation reachable, and unlearning is repeatedly found to suppress rather than erase, the knowledge recoverable by fine-tuning or probing [Łucki et al., 2025, Deeb and Roger, 2025]: accuracy at chance is evidence about behaviour, not about what remains reachable.

What is missing is thus not the will to differentiate but a per-principal mechanism inside one set of weights and an algebra for it, since authority is useful because it composes: a principal under two restrictions holds their conjunction, and principals pooling outputs command their union. The shape of that algebra is classical – Denning’s lattice model combines security requirements by greatest lower bound [Denning, 1976], and non-interference says what unavailability means [Goguen and Meseguer, 1982] – and so is the debt: such properties are notoriously not preserved under composition [McCullough, 1988], and which strengthened conditions survive, at what price, is literature of its own [McLean, 1994, Mantel, 2002]. LLM capability profile lattice inherits both. We define capability-gated deployment (Fig. 1a): per-principal access control inside one set of weights, varying only one privilege level per control coordinate. Configurations form a finite distributive lattice: a principal under several restrictions holds the meet, a coalition pooling outputs commands the join (Fig. 1b). We instantiate it by sparse rank gating over a nested factorisation, steer profile search with a one-pass attribution, and read every result once from a pre-registered held-out split — an A/B discipline portable to any capability-control evaluation. Security composes at meets (provably under a monotone-elicitation assumption we then falsify pointwise, and empirically in two lineages) while utility does not: individually harmless profiles compose to real retention and fluency damage, and no compositional bound is possible. 

Scope. All claims assume query access (outputs observed at one’s own configuration, identity bound at the gateway, weights untouched) in expectation over a declared elicitation distribution and against a declared probe class; the gap from these expectation-based guarantees to worst-case, differential-privacy-style separation is the open problem we pose.

Direction in the lattice decides what survives composition (proofs in Appendix A). Under a monotone-elicitation assumption (Assumption A.4) (which §3 finds violated only pointwise, by about a point) security composes: separation survives every further meet (Theorem A.5). Coalition pooling outputs realises the join of its members’ profiles, so (3) imposes separation there (Theorem A.6). Collateral admits no compositional bound: zero-collateral profiles can meet to lose every retained item under exactly additive margins, since accuracy thresholds a margin (Theorem A.7). Nothing so far has named a mechanism, and nothing needed to: any construction offering indepen-dently addressable sites with ordered, reachability-nested settings instantiates Definition 2.1, and everything above applies to it. 

2.3 Instantiating the Interface Mechanism. We instantiate Definition 2.1 with the nested subspace networks of Rauba and van der Schaar [2026], as Rauba et al. [2026] deploy them for least-privilege inference: there |P| = 1 ,

Su = ∅, and one scalar rank minimises privilege subject to a utility floor. We add configurations off that diagonal, and principals in the plural (App. B.1 details the correspondence). A control coordinate is one of each block’s MLP projections, reparameterised as W ≈ BA ; levels are ranks, and level r

keeps the prefix factors (Fig. 2a), so with θ the post-adaptation parameters, Tc⊤ (θ) = θ holds exactly. On Qwen3-1.7B, ten levels on each of |K| = 84 coordinates give |C| = 10 84 . Composition is then exact at the level of reachable computation: nested factors carry meets to intersected write-spaces and joins to their sums (Theorem A.3), which extends Theorem A.5 from outputs to representations. The mechanism supplies every property the framework consumes — reachability monotone (2), degradation gradual and differential , sensitivity concentrated in few coordinates, tensor shapes unchanged — each itemised, with what it buys the framework, in App. B.1.

## 3 Results 

We study the framework and our instantiation on Qwen3-1.7B [Qwen Team, 2025] and repeat the pipeline verbatim on SmolLM2-1.7B [Allal et al., 2025]. 

Protocol. All headline numbers are read once from held-out split B, with the split-A value beside them; accuracies carry Wilson intervals throughout. The terms of (5) (and through it Sep and Ret 

of (3)) are measured on three datasets. The suppress set Su is WMDP [Li et al., 2024], all three domains: bio ( 1273 items), chem ( 408 ), cyber ( 1987 ). The retain set ¯Ru is seven MMLU subjects [Hendrycks et al., 2021] pooled into two macros, each scored as total correct over total items: adjacent 

(college biology, chemistry, and computer science, one per hazard domain) and distant (philosophy, high-school US history, international law, and high-school macroeconomics). The fluency price Φ,the 1.10 × gate, and every perplexity ratio in the paper are measured on MiniPile [Kaddour, 2023], a distillation of the Pile [Gao et al., 2020]. Every benchmark is frozen into splits A and B before any measurement (§2.4). Each substrate is adapted once by the recipe of §2.3. The well-formedness check of Rauba et al. [2026] (held-out perplexity stable across the rank ladder where naive SVD truncation collapses) passes under our mixed recipe (Fig. 8, App. B.1), and so does the check that is ours, usability off the diagonal against the number of gated coordinates (Fig. 9). A domain enters for a substrate only if, on the screening half, c⊤ accuracy clears αD by 5 points with the Wilson lower bound clear of it, lest every configuration would vacuously separate.

B.1 Instantiation and substrate validation What the mechanism supplies. Monotone reachability (2) is the nested subspace property [Rauba and van der Schaar, 2026]. After nested adaptation, degradation is gradual where naive truncation collapses (this makes searching C feasible at all) and it is differential : hard instances lose first, which is what lets a configuration below c⊤ separate one domain yet retain another [Rauba et al., 2026]. Subject-specific sensitivity concentrates in a few coordinates [Rauba et al., 2026], making the admissible set of Definition 2.2 plausibly non-empty. And tensor shapes are preserved, so the interface fits a pretrained transformer unchanged [Rauba and van der Schaar, 2026]. 

The adaptation recipe. Each step forwards the batch twice, once at the anchor c⊤ and once at the sampled variant, combining the two losses under learned per-level uncertainty weights [Rauba et al., 2026]. A mixed variant is weighted by the mean of its levels’ uncertainties, and global and mixed draws alternate with equal probability (Fig. 2b). 

Relation to prior work. The nested subspace networks of Rauba and van der Schaar [2026] set one global rank and name the layer-specific case as open; in Rauba et al. [2026], per-coordinate configurations appear only as a search space. The lattice of §2.1 is that vector-valued generalisation, with its algebra — meets, joins, composition laws. Conditioning the allocation φ on request-time signals would recover the per-request allocator of Rauba et al. [2026]; the experiments here use static per-principal profiles.

B.2 Objective and attribution details The objective’s terms. Suppression is rewarded at weight λ, clamped at αD , so the term’s optimum is accuracy at chance. Retention is measured on ¯Ru, the retain set pooled into groups M large enough for the per-task tolerance to be measurable ( τ = 0 .01 is half an item on a fifty-item subject); the hinge (the collateral γRu of (3) made concrete) charges drops beyond τ at a rate ρ no suppression can repay. Φ( c) applies the linearisation of (6) to held-out next-token loss rather than the margin, predicting each gated coordinate’s fluency cost; it sums these predictions over supp( c), negatives clipped, at exchange rate ν.

Constants. As recorded in the configuration of the reported run: λ = 2 (lambda_suppress ), 

ρ = 100 (penalty_scale ), ν = 1 (fluency_price_weight ), τ = 0 .01 (eps_preserve_drop ), and αD = 0 .25 for four-option items. The fluency gate, max_ppl_ratio = 1 .10 , sits outside (5) as a hard constraint on candidates and is what makes most configurations ineligible regardless of score. 

Φ( c) is recorded as the sum over supp( c) of the clipped first-order singleton ∆NLL, so ν weights a summed ∆NLL, not a perplexity ratio. The search’s winner is the lower quartile of B = 1000 

bootstrap resamples of the objective, not the argmax. 

Attribution’s lineage and use. Equation (6) is gradient-times-activation [Shrikumar et al., 2017] at the factorisation bottleneck — equivalently the first-order Taylor criterion for structure removal [Molchanov et al., 2019], or attribution patching with a zero baseline [Nanda, 2023, Syed et al., 2024]. The prediction picks the levels the measured sweep evaluates and a fixed rescue quota of coordinates by predicted selectivity — a set-difference contrast against the retained tasks, after Wei et al. [2024]. Concretely, the search takes 24 coordinates at four ranks each with a rescue quota of six, sweep fractions 0.75 , 0.5, 0.375 , 0.25 , 0.125 , 0.0625 , and carries 40 finalists into gate verification; the shortlist is not a parameter of this stage, being E2’s frozen twelve-coordinate output per strategy, which the search consumes as given. Re-run against held-out next-token loss, the same pass yields the per-coordinate prices Φ sums in (5). 

Instrument validation. The gating identity is exact to fp32, and first-order predictions order measured drops (Spearman ρ = 0 .61 , sign agreement 0.92 ) while increasingly underestimating them with intervention size — an ordering signal, never effect sizes (Fig. 10a). A measurement concern. The full-rank model answers only 43 .2% of cyber questions correctly, against 67 .6% for bio and 53 .4% for chem. An intervention cannot flip an answer that was already wrong, so cyber may appear harder to suppress simply because fewer cyber items are known at baseline. We therefore repeat the comparison using only baseline-correct questions. To keep the domains comparable, we retain 87 items from each, answer letters balanced and positions matched in each domain’s baseline-margin ordering. For every in-gate singleton, we ask how much of the matched items’ original decision margin it removes and how often it changes a known answer to a wrong one (Fig. 13).

The narrower claim. Together, the raw and matched analyses establish a narrower claim than cyber being intrinsically inseparable. The present MLP-prefix interface has less leverage over cyber than over the bio–chem average, but this is not a uniform cyber deficit: baseline-known cyber and bio questions respond similarly, while chemistry is unusually susceptible. The singleton grid still makes a pure search-failure explanation unlikely, while leaving four mechanistic possibilities, each with a separable prediction. Distributed support predicts a lower concentration of positive singleton effects and a gradual catch-up under measured top-k accumulation. Rank-order misalignment predicts cyber sensitivity in the retained low-index factor bands when non-prefix bands are ablated at matched NLL. 

Computation outside the MLP surface predicts disproportionate cyber leverage from attention-head or residual-stream interventions. Overlap with general capability predicts that cyber suppression lies on a worse code/technical-utility Pareto frontier.
